# Intrepid Realtors — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Nairobi Quiet Luxury
Very restrained editorial luxury: deep navy, warm ivory, and muted brass with architectural photography and generous whitespace. The tone is established, calm, and quietly confident.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Urban Terracotta Modernism
A warmer Nairobi-inspired direction using clay, sand, ink, and botanical green, with tactile surfaces and bolder editorial blocks. The tone is local, human, and contemporary.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Monochrome Estate Journal
A black-and-white editorial system with sharp typography, oversized image crops, and small brass accents. The tone is precise, premium, and magazine-like.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Nairobi Quiet Luxury

### Design Movement
Contemporary African editorial modernism: a blend of boutique hospitality branding, architectural publishing, and refined Nairobi urbanism.

### Core Principles
1. **Composed confidence:** Strong hierarchy and decisive spacing make the brand feel established without making unverifiable claims.
2. **Editorial clarity:** Photography, typography, and short copy do most of the persuasion; decoration stays purposeful.
3. **Warm precision:** Deep navy and charcoal create trust, while brass and ivory soften the experience.
4. **Local intelligence:** Nairobi neighbourhoods, KES pricing, and practical property details are treated as first-class content.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is anchored by midnight navy (#0d1b2a) and ink charcoal (#18242c) to signal trust and permanence. Ivory (#f7f5ef) gives the interface breathing room and a premium print-like quality. Brass (#c69b5a) is used sparingly for wayfinding, badges, and key calls to action so it feels ownable rather than ornamental.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetrical editorial rhythm: a split hero with an offset search panel, alternating image-and-copy bands, and property grids that break occasionally into a feature card. Content should feel composed like a property journal rather than stacked like a SaaS landing page.

### Signature Elements
- A slim brass rule and small uppercase eyebrow labels to frame major sections.
- Ivory paper-like sections alternating with midnight navy bands.
- A geometric “I” monogram mark made from two offset architectural bars.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a well-made property brochure: clear, tactile, and quick. Buttons respond with a subtle lift and brass shift; cards reveal a slightly richer image crop and a clear action without noisy effects.

### Animation
Use 180–240ms ease-out transitions for buttons, links, filters, and image crops. On first load, reveal major sections with a gentle upward opacity transition, staggered by 50ms. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and never animate layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for large editorial headlines and **Manrope** for body copy, controls, metadata, and navigation. Headlines should be confident and compact; body copy should remain highly readable at 16–18px. Use uppercase tracking for labels and property metadata.

### Brand Essence
Intrepid Realtors helps Nairobi buyers, renters, owners, and investors make clearer property decisions through responsive, locally grounded guidance.

**Personality:** Assured, discerning, responsive.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and human. CTAs are specific and low-pressure. Microcopy is transparent about sample listings and next steps.

- “Find a place you’ll be proud to call home.”
- “Tell us what you’re looking for. We’ll help you explore the right direction.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is set in a high-contrast serif with “INTREPID” carrying the visual weight and “REALTORS” in small spaced capitals beneath. The icon is a no-text geometric I monogram built from two offset architectural bars, suggesting a doorway and a decisive path.

### Signature Brand Color
**Intrepid Brass — #C69B5A.** It should appear as a thin rule, selected state, icon accent, or focused CTA detail—not as a dominant fill everywhere.
